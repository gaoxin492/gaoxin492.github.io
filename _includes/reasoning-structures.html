Reasoning Representations and Structures

  • Continuous and Latent

    Learned latent states and probability distributions that evolve across reasoning steps.

  • Discrete and Structured

    Executable code, graphs, and symbolic expressions that organize reasoning into explicit steps.

  • Visual and Spatial

    Images and diagrams that encode dense visual information and spatial relationships.

  • Iterative and Recurrent

    Repeated computation and refinement, including looped architectures and diffusion-based processes.

These possibilities are complementary, not separate tracks. Reasoning can combine different representations and computational structures.
